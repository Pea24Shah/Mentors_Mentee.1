How matching works

  1. Everyone answers a short survey. Students and mentors describe their interests, majors, style and availability.
  2. We compare every student with every mentor. A match of 50% or more is made automatically with the best mentor still free.
  3. Staff handle the rest. When no mentor reaches 50%, staff choose between the two closest, so every student with a possible mentor gets one.
